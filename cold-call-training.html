Chapter 3

Your Process — Step by Step

You'll receive a spreadsheet with leads from our lead engine. Each lead has a score: HOT, WARM, or COLD. We prioritize HOT leads first. Here's exactly what you do:

Review the Lead

Open the spreadsheet. Look at the lead's name, business name, city, phone, category, and score. If they have no website (or a weak one) = strong opportunity. If they already have a good website = pitch a redesign or SEO/ads instead.

Research (Quick)

Before calling, Google the business name + city. Check their Google Business Profile. Look at their reviews. Check if they have a website. Spend 30-60 seconds max. This gives you personalized context for the call.

Make the Call

Call the lead phone number. Introduce yourself from TKVibes. Be warm, professional, and confident. Use the script below as a guide — adapt it naturally. Keep it conversational, not robotic.

Show the Sample Website

If they're interested, offer to send them a sample website link via WhatsApp. We have 5 sample websites in our portfolio. Send the link and ask them to take 2 minutes to view it. Follow up in 10 minutes.

Ask Qualification Questions

After they've seen the sample or during the conversation, ask the qualification questions (see Chapter 6). You need to determine: are they interested? Do they have budget? Are they the decision-maker? What's their timeline?

Qualify or Disqualify

Based on answers, mark the lead as Qualified or Not Interested in the spreadsheet. A qualified lead = interested, has budget, can make decisions, and has a reasonable timeline (next 1-4 weeks).

Handoff to CEO

Update the spreadsheet with your notes. Tag it for CEO follow-up. The CEO will take over qualified leads and close the deal. Your job is qualification only — not closing.

HOT = Score 70+ — Excellent prospect, call first WARM = Score 45-69 — Good prospect, call second COLD = Score <45 — Low priority

Chapter 4

Cold Calling Script

This is a guide — not a word-for-word script. Adapt your tone to the conversation. Be warm, be real, be human. The goal is to qualify, not to close.

📞 PHASE 1: Opening (15 seconds)

Your Opening
YOU "Hi, is this [business name]? Am I speaking with [owner/manager name]?"
— Wait for confirmation, introduce yourself in a friendly tone —
YOU "Hi [name], this is [your name] from TKVibes. We're a web design studio based in Delhi. I came across your business online — [mention something specific: good reviews, great location, etc.] — and I noticed you don't have a strong website presence yet. I thought I'd reach out to see if that's something you've been thinking about?"

Why This Works

You lead with a compliment (noticed their business), then state the pain point (no website/weak presence), then ask an open question. This feels helpful, not salesy. Never say "I'm calling to sell you..."

💬 PHASE 2: Conversation Flow

If They Say "I Already Have a Website"
YOU "That's great! How long ago was it built? Is it bringing you the results you want? Most business owners I talk to say their site feels outdated or doesn't bring in enough enquiries. We specialize in refreshing older sites to make them mobile-friendly, faster, and better at converting visitors into customers."
YOU "Would you be open to a quick look? We can do a free audit and share what improvements could help you get more leads."
If They Say "I Don't Need a Website" or "We Do Fine Without One"
YOU "I completely understand, and I'm sure word of mouth has been working well for you. But here's what I see happening — when people search for [service they offer] in [their city], the first thing they do is Google. If they can't find a professional website, they often go to a competitor who has one. A simple 3-page site can be up in a matter of days and costs less than what most businesses spend on print ads. Would it make sense to at least see what it would look like?"
If They Say "Tell Me More" or Seem Interested
YOU "Great! So here's what we do — we build professional, modern websites for service businesses like yours. We handle everything: the design, the content, getting it online, and making sure it shows up on Google. We have different packages depending on what you need — from a simple 3-page starter site at ₹9,999, all the way to a full 5-page site with SEO and branding at ₹14,999. I'd love to show you an example of something we've done for a similar business. Can I send you a link on WhatsApp so you can take a look?"

🔑 PHASE 3: Qualification Questions

After They've Seen the Sample or Expressed Interest, Ask:
YOU "Just so I understand your situation better —"
YOU "1. Are you the person who makes the decisions on things like this, or would someone else need to be involved?"
YOU "2. Have you ever had a website before, or would this be your first one?"
YOU "3. Roughly, what kind of timeline were you thinking? Are you looking to get something done this month, or is this more of a future plan?"
YOU "4. Do you have a budget in mind for this? Our packages start at ₹9,999 — does that feel reasonable for what you need?"

Listen More Than You Talk

The best qualification happens when you ask questions and let them talk. Their answers tell you everything: budget, timeline, pain level, decision power. If they're not the decision-maker, ask when the decision-maker will be available and call back.

🎯 PHASE 4: Closing the Call

Qualified Lead — Handoff
YOU "That's perfect. Let me share your details with our team lead who handles new projects. His name is Tarun and he'll reach out to you to discuss next steps in detail and give you a custom proposal. Expect to hear from him within 24 hours. In the meantime, I'll send you a WhatsApp message with our portfolio links. Does that work for you?"
Not Interested / Not Now
YOU "No problem at all, I completely understand. Would it be okay if I check back with you in a couple of months? Sometimes the timing isn't right now, but things change. I'll leave my number with you — [your number] — feel free to reach out anytime."
Then update the spreadsheet: mark as "Follow-up (2 months)" and add your notes.

DO NOT

  • Do NOT argue with the lead if they say no. Be polite and move on.
  • Do NOT promise discounts or change prices — only your CEO can do that.
  • Do NOT try to close the deal yourself. Your job is to qualify only.
  • Do NOT make up features or capabilities. If you don't know, say "I'll check with our team and get back to you."
  • Do NOT be pushy. A relaxed, helpful tone closes more leads than a hard sell.

Chapter 6

Handling Objections

You will hear the same objections again and again. Here's exactly how to respond. Practice these till they feel natural.

ObjectionYour Response
"I already have a website." "Great! When was it built? Most older sites don't work well on mobile and lose potential customers. We specialize in redesigning existing sites — making them faster, mobile-friendly, and better at converting visitors into calls or bookings. Would you like a free audit?"
"It's too expensive." "I understand. Our Starter package is just ₹9,999 — that's a complete 3-page website with logo design, Google Business setup, and SEO. Think of it as a one-time investment that brings you customers for years. Most businesses recover that cost from a single new client."
"I don't have time right now." "Totally fair. We handle everything — you just need to share a few details about your business. The whole process takes very little of your time. Could we start with a 5-minute chat to see if it makes sense? If the timing really isn't right, I'll check back in a couple of months."
"We rely on word of mouth / walk-ins." "Word of mouth is the best kind of marketing! But here's what happens — when someone hears about you from a friend, the first thing they do is Google your business. If they find nothing or a bad site, they second-guess their decision. A simple website gives those referrals confidence and helps you capture them."
"Send me an email / website link first." "Of course! Can I send it to your WhatsApp? It's faster and I can follow up with any questions you have. What's your number?" — Get the WhatsApp number, send the portfolio link, and follow up in 10-15 minutes.
"I need to discuss with my partner." "Absolutely, that makes sense. When would be a good time to call back when you both can discuss? Is tomorrow at [time] good? I can also send our pricing and portfolio over WhatsApp so you can review it together."
"How do I know you're legit?" "Great question. You can check our work at tkvibes.com — we have 5+ live client websites you can browse. We're also on WhatsApp at +91 98182 46938. We've delivered 50+ projects with 98% satisfaction. I can also share references of businesses we've worked with if you'd like."
"Can you do [specific feature]?" "Most likely yes! We build custom features all the time — booking systems, payment gateways, admin dashboards, API integrations. If you're not sure, I'll note it down and our tech team can confirm. What exactly did you have in mind?"

Golden Rule of Objections

An objection is NOT a rejection — it's a question hiding behind a statement. "Too expensive" might mean "I don't understand the value yet." "Not interested" might mean "You caught me at a bad time." Don't argue. Explore politely. If they truly aren't interested, thank them and move on. Time wasted on a "no" is time you could spend finding a "yes."

Chapter 7

What Makes a Lead Qualified?

After your call, you need to decide if this lead is worth passing to the CEO. A qualified lead meets ALL of these criteria:

✅ Qualified Lead Checklist

  • Showed genuine interest — they listened, asked questions, engaged with the conversation
  • Viewed or will view sample work — they agreed to see our portfolio or have seen it
  • Decision maker reached — you spoke to the owner, manager, or someone with authority to approve spending
  • Has budget — they didn't balk at ₹9,999-₹14,999 range; they indicated they're open to spending
  • Reasonable timeline — they want to start within 1-4 weeks, or at least are open to discussing soon
  • Contact info confirmed — you have their working WhatsApp number and/or email
  • Agreed to follow-up — they're okay with the CEO calling/contacting them

❌ Red Flags (Not Qualified)

  • Not the decision maker — and the DM is not available or hard to reach
  • No budget — "I can't afford anything right now" or "we have zero budget"
  • Not interested — firm "no" with no opening for follow-up
  • No timeline — "Maybe next year" or "not anytime soon" with no specific plan
  • Going with someone else — already hired another agency or developer
  • Rude or hostile — if they're aggressive or abusive, just end the call politely

Chapter 8

Passing the Baton — Handoff to CEO

Your job ends when the lead is qualified. Here's exactly how the handoff works:

Update the Spreadsheet

Mark the lead as Qualified. Add detailed notes about the conversation — what package they were interested in, what they liked/disliked, any specific requirements they mentioned, and the best time for the CEO to call.

Send a Brief to CEO

Send a message to the CEO (Tarun) with the lead's details: name, business, phone, what was discussed, and the recommended next step. E.g.: "Dr. Mehta from Mehta Dental Clinic, Gurgaon. Very interested in Growth package. Saw sample and loved it. Best time to call: 11am tomorrow. His WhatsApp is same number."

Send Portfolio to Lead (if not already done)

If you didn't already send the sample during the call, send it now via WhatsApp with a brief note: "Hi [name], here's the sample site I mentioned. Feel free to have a look — our CEO Tarun will follow up with you tomorrow. Thanks for your time!"

CEO Closes the Deal

The CEO takes over from here. Your qualification work made it easy for him to go in and close. You get credit for every lead that converts.

Why Your Role Matters

You are the front line. Every qualified lead you find is a potential ₹10,000-₹70,000+ project for TKVibes. Your warm introduction makes it 10x easier for the CEO to close the deal. A cold lead who never gets called = ₹0. A qualified lead you hand off = a real chance at new business.

Quick Reference

🚀 Final Tips for Success

Be Warm & Human

Nobody wants to talk to a robot. Smile while you talk — it changes your voice tone. Use the person's name. Listen actively. A genuine conversation beats a perfect script every time.

Speed Matters

Call HOT leads first. The faster you call after a lead is generated, the more likely they are to remember you. Same-day contact has the highest conversion rate.

Keep Learning

Note down what works and what doesn't. Share feedback with the team. If you find a new objection we haven't covered, log it — we'll add it to the training.

Volume = Results

Expect 5-10 calls to get 1 good conversation, and 3-5 conversations to get 1 qualified lead. It's a numbers game. Don't get discouraged by rejections — every "no" gets you closer to a "yes."

Stay Compliant

Respect DND (Do Not Disturb) numbers. If someone asks to be removed from your list, note it immediately and never call them again. Keep calls professional and within reasonable hours (10am-7pm).

Believe in the Product

We build genuinely good websites at fair prices. You're not selling something useless — you're helping real businesses grow. Confidence in what you offer comes through on every call.
